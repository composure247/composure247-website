Support

Support

A real person will help. Here's how to reach us.

If you use the Composure247 app or platform and something isn't working, or you just have a question, we're glad to help.

Email support

We answer support email during UK business hours, Monday to Friday.

support@composure247.com

Getting help quickly

To help us sort things out on the first reply, it's useful to include:

  • the name of your MSP or workspace
  • your browser and device
  • what you were doing and what happened

Account access

If you're on an MSP's team, your admin creates your account. To start a new workspace, go to app.composure247.com/signup. If you can't sign in or need your access changed, your MSP admin is usually the fastest route and can sort most account issues directly. For anything they can't, email us and we'll help.
