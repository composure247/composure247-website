How MSPs use Composure

Six scenarios Composure was built for

Hover any scenario to see the pain today and how Composure solves it.

1

Major incident response

A critical outage hits multiple clients while engineers work under pressure, exactly when nobody has time to write client updates.

Hover for detail
The pain today

Engineers are heads-down on resolution. Client communications are delayed, inconsistent, or damaging, written manually under extreme pressure.

How Composure solves it

The moment a P1 is raised, Composure drafts role-calibrated messages for every stakeholder, schedules holding updates, and briefs Account Managers, with no engineering involvement.

Business impact

Engineers stay focused on resolution. Clients receive timely updates. AMs are never caught off guard.

2

Planned maintenance and migrations

Maintenance windows, migrations, and cutovers need careful, multi-round communication before, during, and after the event.

Hover for detail
The pain today

Maintenance comms are written ad hoc, often late, and inconsistent. Contacts miss the notice, and the completion confirmation rarely gets sent.

How Composure solves it

Build a full sequence, notice, reminder, work beginning, completion, and schedule it in advance. Every contact gets the right message at the right time.

Business impact

No client is caught off guard. Your team spends minutes, not hours, on maintenance comms.

3

Keeping Account Managers ahead

When a client calls their Account Manager mid-incident and the AM does not know what is happening, trust collapses instantly.

Hover for detail
The pain today

AMs are the last to know. They have no talking points, no status, and no context, making a bad situation worse.

How Composure solves it

Composure briefs AMs the moment client comms go out, status, messages sent, talking points, and engineer contact, in one secure internal update.

Business impact

AMs handle client calls with confidence. Client trust holds even under pressure.

4

Post-incident reporting

One of the most commercially important communications an MSP produces, and the most consistently neglected.

Hover for detail
The pain today

Reports are written days later by engineers who have already moved on, inconsistent, superficial, and sometimes never sent at all.

How Composure solves it

Composure drafts a professional report the moment an incident closes, from the full timeline and communication log, with a structured approval workflow.

Business impact

Every incident ends with a timely, professional report that builds client confidence.

5

Enterprise client communication

Enterprise clients have multiple stakeholders with different roles and information needs. A single communication style serves none of them.

Hover for detail
The pain today

Most MSPs send one email to all contacts. The Finance Director gets jargon, the CTO gets vague reassurance, and neither is served.

How Composure solves it

Composure tailors each message by role, the CTO gets strategic context, the IT Manager gets technical detail, the Finance Director gets plain-English impact.

Business impact

Enterprise clients feel communicated with specifically. This is often the difference in winning contracts.

6

Reducing churn

Most MSP churn is not technical, it is the feeling that the MSP does not care or is not on top of things.

Hover for detail
The pain today

Clients leave at renewal citing communication issues, even when the technical service was excellent.

How Composure solves it

Consistent, professional communication at every touchpoint builds a perception of reliability that makes you the obvious choice to stay with.

Business impact

Lower churn rate. Higher NPS. Stronger renewals.
