Privacy Policy

How Composure247 handles your data, written from what the app actually does, not from a template.

Last updated 17 October 2026

This policy explains what the Composure247 iOS app and platform do with data, who is responsible for it, and the rights you have. We've kept it in plain English and specific to how the app actually works.

Who we are

Composure247 is operated by CITSYS Limited, registered in England and Wales, company number 07254892 ("CITSYS", "we", "us"). Composure247 is incident-communication software for managed service providers (MSPs). The iOS app is a companion to the platform and is used by an MSP's own staff: engineers, incident managers and account managers. It is not used by the MSPs' own customers.

For any question about this policy or your data, email us at keith@composure247.com.

Two kinds of data, two different responsibilities

This distinction matters, especially if you're reviewing us as part of a security or procurement assessment, so we'll be precise about it.

Data we control. The accounts that let MSP staff use Composure247: a person's name, email address and the roles assigned to them. MSP administrators create these accounts; there is no self sign-up. For this data, CITSYS is the data controller.

Data we process for your MSP. Everything about incidents and clients that lives in a workspace: incidents, internal notes, client-facing updates, notification records, post-incident reports, maintenance windows, and the customer contacts attached to them. Your MSP decides what goes in and why; we only process it on their instructions. For this data, your MSP is the controller and CITSYS is the processor.

If you're an end customer of an MSP and want to exercise rights over incident data, your MSP is the right first point of contact, and we'll support them in responding.

What the app accesses, and why

When you sign in, the app reads from your own Composure247 workspace so you can work from your phone. Specifically, it reads:

  • organisation settings, such as escalation thresholds and role labels
  • team members: name, email address and roles
  • incidents, internal incident notes and client-facing updates
  • notification records and post-incident reports
  • maintenance windows and the customer contacts attached to them

It reads this so it can show you current incidents and let you respond while you're away from a desk.

The app writes exactly one thing. When you answer a chase, it adds an internal note to that incident and updates the incident's "last updated" time. Nothing else in the app writes anything, and nothing the app writes reaches an end customer without a person reviewing and approving it first.

What's stored on your phone

The app keeps a small amount of data on the device itself:

  • Your sign-in session, held in the iOS Keychain and marked device-only. It is excluded from iCloud and iTunes/Finder backups and cannot be read while your phone is locked.
  • Chase answers you give with no signal, kept on the device until a connection is available and they can send.
  • Your appearance choice, whether you prefer light or dark mode.

All of this is cleared when you sign out.

Microphone and speech

One screen lets you answer by voice. The microphone is only active while you hold the "hold to speak" button, and it stops the moment you release it. Speech is turned into text on the device itself (the app requires on-device recognition). Your audio is never sent to Apple, to us, or to anyone else, and it isn't kept once your words appear on screen.

No tracking. No analytics. No third parties.

This is deliberate, and we think it's worth stating plainly. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers of any kind. We do not sell or share your data, and there is no third-party service sitting in the middle. The app communicates only with your own Composure247 workspace and nowhere else. The one external thing bundled into the app is a typeface, which ships inside the app rather than being fetched from the internet.

Where your data is stored

Your Composure247 workspace is hosted on Supabase in the London region (eu-west-2). Your incident data and your account data are stored and processed in the United Kingdom.

Lawful basis

  • For the staff account data we control, our lawful basis is legitimate interests: operating, securing and supporting the Composure247 service for the MSP that employs you, and performing our contract with that MSP.
  • For the incident and client data we process on your MSP's behalf, your MSP determines the lawful basis as controller; we act on their documented instructions under our data processing terms.

How long we keep it, and account closure

We keep workspace data, incidents, notes, updates and related records, for as long as the workspace is active, because it is the MSP's operational record. When an MSP closes their account, we delete their workspace data within 30 days. An MSP can also ask us to delete specific data at any time, and we'll do so once we've confirmed the request is genuine. Data held on your phone is cleared as soon as you sign out, as described above.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy
  • have inaccurate data corrected
  • have your data deleted
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • receive your data in a portable form
  • complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK regulator, at ico.org.uk

To exercise any of these, email keith@composure247.com. If your request is about incident data your MSP controls, we'll point you to your MSP and help them respond. We'll reply to rights requests within one month.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the version here and change the "last updated" date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app or let your MSP administrator know, rather than relying on you to notice.
