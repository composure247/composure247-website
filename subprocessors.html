The companies we use to provide Composure247, what each does, and where.

Version 1.230 October 2026

Who handles your data for us

These are the companies CITSYS LIMITED uses to provide Composure247, what each does, and where. Each handles personal data only on our instructions, under a contract that protects it. This list is Annex 3 of our Data Processing Agreement and section 9 of our Terms of Service.

Payments

Stripe takes card payments for Composure247 subscriptions and message credits. It handles our customers' own billing details (the billing contact, billing address, VAT number and card), not the personal data our customers hold in Composure247, so it is not a sub-processor of that data and is not part of this list. Our Privacy Notice explains how it handles billing details.

Changes

We update this page at least 30 days before a new sub-processor starts handling customer personal data, and we email account administrators at the same time. You can object on reasonable data protection grounds, as our Data Processing Agreement (section 6) explains.

Change history
