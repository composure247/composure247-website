7. AI-assisted features

7.1 What the AI does. The Service uses an AI model, provided by Anthropic (section 9), to:

  • draft customer updates, briefings, post-incident reviews and Root Cause Analyses from the information you and your Users enter;
  • check that information for gaps.

7.2 A person approves what customers see. AI-drafted updates, RCAs and maintenance notices are drafts. One of your Users must read and send or approve each one before a customer receives it.

7.3 The two automatic emails. Two customer emails are sent without a person reviewing each one:

  • the "service restored" email, sent when an incident is marked resolved. It uses our fixed wording, filled in with the incident's details. You can choose not to send it when you mark the incident resolved;
  • the final "resolved" email, sent when the monitoring window ends with no recurrence. It uses the template you set in your settings, or our default until you change it.

Before sending either, the AI may lightly tidy the text, for example grammar, or the spacing left by filled-in fields. It is instructed to keep the wording, tone and any timing commitments exactly. If the tidy-up fails, the text is sent as written. Keep your resolved template short and final. If you don't want these emails to reach a customer, you can pause email for your organisation or switch off email for that contact.

7.4 You stay responsible. AI output can be wrong or incomplete. You are responsible for checking anything you send or approve, and for decisions you make using it.

7.5 Your data and AI training. Customer Data sent to the AI provider is used to produce your drafts. Under our agreement with that provider, it is not used to train AI models, and the provider deletes it within 30 days, unless it is flagged for breaking the provider's usage policy or the law requires it to be kept longer.
