1. Who we are and what this notice covers

1.1 Who we are. Composure247 is software provided by CITSYS LIMITED, a company registered in England and Wales with company number 07254892, whose registered office is at 248 Church Lane, Kingsbury, London, NW9 8SL ("we", "us"). We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office as a data controller, registration number ZC283741.

1.2 What this notice covers. It explains how we use personal data when we decide how and why it is used. That is, we are the controller. It covers:

  • people who visit our website or the Composure247 sign-in and sign-up pages;
  • people who sign up for a trial, or contact us about sales, support or anything else;
  • the people at our customers who use Composure247 (their account and sign-in details, and how they use the service);
  • our customers' billing and contract contacts;
  • people who send a message to the Composure247 WhatsApp number.

1.3 What it doesn't cover. Our customers are managed service providers. They use Composure247 to hold details of their own customers and to send them incident updates. For that data, our customer is the controller and we only process it on their instructions, under our Data Processing Agreement. If you received an incident update from a service provider through Composure247, that provider's privacy notice applies, and they are the right people to contact about your data.

1.4 Contact. Email legal@composure247.com about anything in this notice, or write to us at the address above.

2. The personal data we collect

2.1 When you sign up for a trial: your first and last name, work email address, company name, country and, if you give it, your company's size. We record which versions of our Terms of Service and Data Processing Agreement you accepted, and when.

2.2 When you use Composure247: your name, work email address, mobile number if you add one, your role and permissions, your sign-in details (your password is stored only in scrambled, hashed form), your second sign-in step settings, and single sign-on identifiers if your organisation uses Microsoft Entra ID. We also keep records of what happens in your account, such as sign-ins, changes to settings and messages sent, with the time and IP address.

2.3 Technical data: the IP address and browser details sent with every web request. If the app hits an error in your browser, it sends us a short error report so we can fix it. On our website we count visits using Vercel Web Analytics, which records the page you visited, the page or site that sent you, your country, and your browser, operating system and device type (section 7.4).

2.4 To protect the sign-up page from abuse: a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, so we can limit how many sign-ups come from one place, and the result of the "not a robot" check run by Cloudflare.

2.5 When you contact us: what you tell us, and your contact details.

2.6 Billing: the name and contact details of the person who handles your account, and billing and payment records. We don't hold card details.

2.7 When you message our WhatsApp number: your phone number, what you send and when. If you reply STOP, we record it and switch off WhatsApp messages to your number for every one of our customers who holds it. If we can tell which of our customers your message was for, we pass it to that customer only, and section 1.3 applies to it. If we can't, only our staff can see it. We delete the message after 90 days (section 6).

2.8 Where it comes from. Mostly from you. Some comes from your employer, when an administrator invites you to Composure247 or connects Microsoft Entra ID to add users automatically.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

7.1 The Composure247 app (app.composure247.com) doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies, and doesn't track you across other websites. It stores a small amount of information in your browser that it needs to work:

  • your signed-in session, so you stay signed in;
  • your display preferences, such as table column widths and which sections are open.

These are strictly necessary, so they don't need your consent. Clearing your browser's site data signs you out and resets them.

7.2 The sign-up page runs Cloudflare's "not a robot" check, which reads signals from your browser to tell people from bots. It is used only for that.

7.3 Our fonts are served from our own site, so loading a page doesn't contact a third party for them.

7.4 Our website (www.composure247.com) uses no cookies, and loads no scripts, fonts or other content from third parties. It counts visits using Vercel Web Analytics, served from our own site. It records the page you visited, the page or site that sent you, your country, and your browser, operating system and device type. It doesn't use cookies or keep your IP address: it recognises repeat visits on the same day by a code made from your request, which is reset every 24 hours, so you can't be followed from one day to the next or across other websites. The website also remembers whether you chose light or dark mode, in your browser. If you fill in our contact form, what you enter is sent to Formspree, which passes it to us (section 4).

7.5 The Composure247 iPhone app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code. It keeps a little data on your phone:

  • your sign-in session, in the iOS Keychain, marked device-only, so it isn't backed up to iCloud and can't be read while your phone is locked;
  • answers you give when you have no signal, until they can be sent;
  • your light or dark mode choice.

All of it is cleared when you sign out. One screen lets you answer by voice: the microphone is on only while you hold the button, and speech is turned into text on the phone itself.

8. Security

We protect your data with:

  • encryption in transit and at rest;
  • each customer's data kept separate inside the database itself;
  • a required second sign-in step for administrators;
  • support access to an account that is time-limited and recorded;
  • regular security testing.

Our security overview at composure247.com/security gives more detail. If you think your account has been compromised, email security@composure247.com straight away.

9. Your rights

9.1 Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • get a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have it corrected if it is wrong;
  • have it deleted, where we have no good reason to keep it;
  • restrict how we use it, or object to our using it on the basis of our legitimate interests;
  • object to marketing at any time, which we will always respect;
  • receive data you gave us in a format you can take elsewhere, where the law allows.

9.2 How to ask. Email legal@composure247.com. We will answer within one month, and it is free. We may need to confirm who you are first.

9.3 If your data came to us through a service provider. For data held in Composure247 on behalf of one of our customers (section 1.3), contact that customer. If you contact us instead, we will pass your request to them.

9.4 Complaints. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

10. Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when how we use personal data changes, and change the date below. If a change matters to you, we will tell account administrators by email or in the app. Earlier versions are kept at composure247.com/legal.
