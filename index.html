The problem

The outage is survivable. The silence is not.

MSPs have invested heavily in tools to detect and fix technical issues. But when something goes wrong, what clients experience is your communication, not your infrastructure. And there is rarely a dedicated system for that.

MSPs go silent during incidents

Engineers are heads-down solving the problem. No one is writing client updates. Clients notice the silence and assume the worst.

Everyone gets the same message

A Finance Director does not need technical detail. A CTO does not need reassurance. One message sent to all is right for none.

Account Managers get caught off guard

When a client calls their AM to ask what's happening, the AM has no idea. That conversation destroys confidence instantly.

Post-incident reports are weak and late

Written under pressure by engineers after the fact, post-incident reports are often inconsistent, delayed, and commercially damaging.

How it works

From incident raised to clients informed, without the scramble

Composure works alongside your existing tools and takes the writing, the timing and the chasing out of client communication. Your team stays in control of every send.

1

Incident raised

Raise an incident in Composure, or let your HaloPSA rules suggest one to the on-call incident manager to confirm. The first update is drafted the moment the incident is raised.

2

AI drafts stakeholder messages

Composure drafts separate, role-calibrated messages for each stakeholder, a plain-English summary for the Finance Director, technical context for the IT Manager, a strategic overview for the CTO. Engineers write a quick note; Composure does the rest.

3

Preview, approve, and send

Your team previews each draft per contact, makes any edits, then sends by email and SMS. A per-customer update clock reminds you before the next update is due and escalates if one is missed. Everything sent is kept in an audit trail.

AI-generated incident communications

Composure drafts professional, plain-English communications the moment an incident is raised. Engineers write a quick note, and Composure turns it into a message for each contact.

Role-calibrated stakeholder messaging

Different messages for different contacts. Your CTO receives strategic context. Your Finance Director receives business impact in plain English. Your IT Manager receives technical detail.

Update clock and holding updates

A per-customer update clock reminds your team before each update is due and pages the escalation contact if one is missed. When there is no news yet, Composure drafts an honest holding update on request.

AM and CSM internal briefings

Account Managers and Customer Success Managers receive an automatic internal briefing after every customer update, so they are never caught off guard when a client calls.

AI post-incident reports

When an incident closes, Composure collects the facts from the engineer and incident manager and drafts the root cause analysis. An independent AI reviewer scores it, a liability check forces executive sign-off on risky wording, and corrective actions are chased to completion. Communicate plan.

Governance, audit trail, and control

Every communication sent and every approval is recorded. A person approves every AI-drafted update before a customer gets it. Admins can pause all email or SMS notifications when a situation is sensitive.

Security & compliance

Built for professional MSPs who take data seriously

Composure is designed with security at its core. Data hosted in the UK, encryption at rest and in transit, an audit trail of communications and approvals, and built for UK GDPR.

UK data hosting

Your data is hosted in the UK (London). Email delivery runs in the EU, and AI drafting and texts are processed in the US. See the sub-processor list.

Encryption at rest and in transit

AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS encryption in transit.

Role-based access & MFA

Role-based permissions. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for administrators, can be required for all staff, and can be turned on per customer for portal users.

Full audit trail

Communications sent, approvals and privileged actions are recorded in an audit trail. Support access is time-limited and recorded.

Built for UK GDPR

Data minimisation, purpose limitation and a published sub-processor list. A data processing agreement is accepted at sign-up.

Approval workflows

A person approves every AI-drafted incident update, maintenance notice and RCA before a customer gets it. The automatic "Service restored" and "Resolved" emails are the only exceptions. RCAs also get an independent AI review and a liability check.
